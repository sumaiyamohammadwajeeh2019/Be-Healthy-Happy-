💡 Health Tips

Daily tips for women

💧 Water First
1 glass after waking up. 8-10 glass / day.
😴 Sleep 7-8 Hours
No sleep = more belly fat. Sleep before 11 PM.
🚶 Walk After Eating
10-15 min after meal. Best for diabetic + PCOS.
🍬 Sugar = Belly Fat
1 cold drink = 10 spoon sugar.
🌸 Period Tip
Period time: Warm food, iron food, rest. Don't do heavy workout. Use heat bag on belly for pain. Track period date every month.
👵 Old Age Tip
After 50: Milk daily, morning sun 15 min, Tai Chi for balance, check pressure + sugar monthly. Don't walk alone at night, keep phone with you.
🇯🇵 Japanese Secret - Hara Hachi Bu
Eat until 80% full, not 100%.
⚠️ Stop If:
Chest pain, dizziness, faint, severe pain. Rest + tell family.
